# Round 1 — general
**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Scope reviewed:** commit `8224719` vs parent — trimming and AOT annotations on the reflection runtime

## Summary

The commit marks both `AddMediator` overloads and the public constructors of `Mediator` and the two exception behaviors with `[RequiresUnreferencedCode]` and `[RequiresDynamicCode]`, backed by polyfills that the trimmer can see, and it caches the closed enumerable service type plus `MethodInfo` once per closed behavior and exception type. Runtime risk is low: the cache still invokes `Handle` / `Execute` with the same arguments, and a factory that throws does not stay in the dictionary. The gap is annotation placement. Those attributes sit on constructors that do not reflect, while `Send`, `Publish`, `CreateStream`, and behavior `Handle` still contain `MakeGenericType`, `Activator.CreateInstance`, `GetMethod`, and `MethodInfo.Invoke` outside any Requires scope. I did not re-run the AOT sample publish. Its project sets `PublishAot`, `IsAotCompatible`, and both analyzers, and neither it nor the repo `NoWarn` lists IL2026 or IL3050.

## Issues

### Issue 1 — Severity: suggestion
- File: source/timewarp-mediator/mediator.cs:46
- Description: `[RequiresUnreferencedCode]` / `[RequiresDynamicCode]` are on the constructors (`mediator.cs:27` and `:37`, `request-exception-processor-behavior.cs:22`, `request-exception-action-processor-behavior.cs:22`) and on both `AddMediator` overloads. That meets the written entry-point list, and `AddMediator` does warn at its call site. It does not cover the reflection. Shipping BCL `RequiresUnreferencedCodeAttribute` / `RequiresDynamicCodeAttribute` (net6 through net9) are `AttributeTargets.Method | Constructor | Class`, `Inherited = false`, with a string constructor argument, `Message`, and `Url`. The polyfill in `internal/trimming-attributes.cs` matches that shape and the namespace `System.Diagnostics.CodeAnalysis`, so ILLink/ILC will see the attributes that were actually applied. `Class` in `AttributeUsage` does not flow a constructor annotation onto other members, and nothing here annotates the class. `Send` (`mediator.cs:46`, `:64`, `:84`), `Publish` (`:120`, `:133`, with the reflection in private `PublishNotification` at `:151`), and both `CreateStream` overloads (`:164`, `:184`) still call `MakeGenericType` and `Activator.CreateInstance`. Both behavior `Handle` methods (`request-exception-processor-behavior.cs:27`, `request-exception-action-processor-behavior.cs:27`) still `MethodInfo.Invoke`, and the `MakeGenericType` / `GetMethod` work lives in `GetHandlerReflection` / `GetActionReflection`. A trimmed or AOT app that reaches those members does not get IL2026/IL3050 at the `IMediator` or `IPipelineBehavior` call. The member bodies are not a Requires scope, so the trimmer still warns there. With default `TrimmerSingleWarn` that shows up as IL2104 on `TimeWarp.Mediator` rather than at the call; `TrimmerSingleWarn=false` shows the IL2026/IL3050 on the library members. `#if !NET5_0_OR_GREATER` and `#if !NET7_0_OR_GREATER` match the `netstandard2.0;net6.0` TFMs (no second `RequiresUnreferencedCodeAttribute` on net6, no second `RequiresDynamicCodeAttribute` on either TFM).
- Suggestion: Keep the `AddMediator` and constructor annotations. Also put both attributes on the three `Send` overloads, both `Publish` overloads, both `CreateStream` overloads, both behavior `Handle` methods, and the private methods that still reflect (`PublishNotification`, `GetHandlerReflection`, `GetActionReflection`). Do not put them on `IMediator` or `IPipelineBehavior`; implementations must match, and the generated mediator would then be marked unsafe too. Calls through those interfaces will still not warn. `AddMediator` remains the call-site signal for the DI path.
- Status: open
